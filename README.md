# Haris Mahmood

I'm an AI research engineer in Islamabad, Pakistan. Most recently I was at [Unify](https://unify.ai) (YC W23), where I built much of the platform under its AI teammates, from the RAG pipeline to the generative UI they acted through and their integrations with a company's apps, and then led a ground-up redesign of its open-source [agent harness](https://github.com/unifyai/unify-agent), with an agent mesh and a memory built for both memoization and generalisation. The new harness solved more Continual-ARC puzzles than the old one and than OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, and Prime Agent. [More on that work](https://harismahmood.dev/work/unify-2025).

Before that I co-founded Ivy and led its source-to-source ML transpiler, which rewrote PyTorch models and libraries as working, readable TensorFlow and JAX, so a PyTorch model could ship to phones through TensorFlow Lite as source an engineer could still edit; its integration was [merged into Kornia](https://github.com/kornia/kornia/pull/3045). I started out at CureMD, training layout-detection and entity-recognition models for document understanding.

I'm looking for my next role: building agents and their harnesses and running them securely and reliably, or post-training the models inside them, which is where I've been spending my time since. [What draws me to it](https://harismahmood.dev/research).
